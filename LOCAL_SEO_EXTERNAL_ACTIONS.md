# AB Service External Local SEO Actions

These actions occur outside the repository. Inspect account state first. Before saving a public/account change, verify the exact field and obtain action-time confirmation.

## Evidence to collect first

- Official registered business name and whether the visible name “AB Service” matches real-world branding.
- Current telephone, email and website URL.
- Whether customers are served at a staffed physical location. If not, the address must be hidden.
- Actual opening/contact hours.
- Municipalities genuinely served in normal operations.
- Current technician recognition numbers and scope.
- Active brands, service limits, air-conditioning/F-gas evidence and insurance/licence evidence where relevant.
- Direct Google Business Profile URL and review-request URL.
- Authentic logo, exterior/service-area imagery and real completed-job photos with consent.

## Google Business Profile

1. Confirm ownership and record the profile ID.
2. Use the real-world business name only; do not add keywords or municipalities to the name.
3. Choose the most specific primary category matching the dominant verified business activity. Likely candidates include plumber or heating contractor, but confirm the actual activity and available Google category before saving.
4. Add secondary categories only for active, evidenced services.
5. Set `https://www.abservice24.be/` as the website URL and use the verified phone.
6. If customers are not received at the address during stated hours, configure a service-area business and hide the address.
7. Add only genuine service areas. Google permits up to 20, but that is not a target; fewer accurate areas are better than keyword coverage.
8. Enter accurate hours and special hours. Do not claim 24/7 availability.
9. Add services using plain descriptions consistent with the website. Do not add fixed prices unless a real maintained price list exists.
10. Upload authentic photos: logo, real technicians with permission, tools, vehicles without misleading location claims and completed work with customer consent.
11. Link the verified profile from the website without copying a stale score or review count.

Official references: [service-area guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en), [address guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2853879?hl=en).

## Legitimate review workflow

1. Ask every eligible customer using the same neutral process after genuine service completion.
2. Send the direct Google review link by email/SMS only with customer permission.
3. Ask for an honest review; do not suggest wording, offer rewards or filter unhappy customers.
4. Do not ask staff, family, suppliers or non-customers.
5. Respond briefly and professionally without revealing work details, address, health, payment or dispute information.
6. Track request date and channel internally, not the desired rating.
7. Keep review schema off the AB Service site. Google treats first-party LocalBusiness/Organization review markup as self-serving.

## Citation cleanup

Create a spreadsheet of genuine listings. For each, record listing URL, displayed name, phone, website, address visibility, status, login owner and correction date. Correct old domains, numbers, keyword-stuffed names and obsolete addresses. Do not create bulk low-quality directory profiles.

Priority sources may include recognized Belgian trade/business directories, suppliers, professional memberships and relevant local organisations only when the listing is genuine.

## Google Search Console

1. Confirm a Domain property for `abservice24.be` using DNS verification.
2. Also inspect URL-prefix properties for `https://www.abservice24.be/`, non-www and HTTP variants if historical data exists.
3. Submit only `https://www.abservice24.be/sitemap.xml`.
4. Check Page Indexing, canonicals, HTTPS and Core Web Vitals after deployment.
5. Inspect the homepage and representative service pages; request indexing only after the production deployment is verified.
6. Export query/page data monthly and segment brand versus non-brand queries.
7. Investigate duplicate/canonical warnings before creating redirects or new pages.
8. Remove any legacy sitemap submitted by `next-sitemap` once the new sitemap is live.

## Bing Webmaster Tools

1. Import the verified Search Console property or verify by DNS.
2. Submit the canonical sitemap.
3. Review crawl errors, indexed pages and backlink data monthly.
4. Do not enable automatic URL submission until deployment behaviour is confirmed.

## Apple Business Connect

1. Claim the real business entity.
2. Match name, phone, website, category and address visibility to the verified Google/website facts.
3. Use service-area settings where supported; do not publish a private or non-customer-facing address.
4. Add only authentic photos and accurate hours.

## Paid search and Local Services Ads

- Confirm Belgian availability and category eligibility before planning Local Services Ads.
- Confirm all licence, recognition, insurance, background-check and service-area requirements before applying.
- Build ad groups around confirmed services, not unsupported “24/7”, “within 60 minutes”, air-conditioning or municipality claims.
- Use landing pages that match the ad intent: maintenance, repair, inspection, gas boilers, sanitary or tariffs.
- Configure call/form conversions without importing personal data into analytics.
- Add negative keywords for jobs not accepted and locations outside the actual service area.
- Do not start paid traffic until form delivery and call handling are operationally tested.

## Backlink opportunities

- Genuine supplier or manufacturer directory listings where eligibility is proven.
- Professional associations or recognition registers tied to a verified person/business.
- Local community sponsorships or partnerships that actually occur.
- Useful technical explanations cited by property managers or local organisations.
- Authentic project case studies partners may reference with customer permission.

Avoid paid link packages, reciprocal-link networks, fake guest posts and location directories created only for ranking.

## Social and video

Repurpose real work evidence into short before/process/after posts only after privacy review. Blur house numbers, faces, documents, screens and identifiable interiors unless explicit permission covers publication. Link back to the relevant service or genuine project page.
