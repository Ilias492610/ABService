## Purpose and boundary

The website and Google Business Profile serve different purposes. The website records the
owner-confirmed operational coverage of all 285 Flemish and 19 Brussels municipalities. It does
not imply 304 branches or local-pack visibility in every municipality.

Google currently permits up to 20 service areas and advises that the overall boundary normally
should not extend much beyond about two hours' driving time from the business base. The official
rule is documented in [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en).

Do not copy all website coverage into the profile. Do not save any list until the real operating
base, routine travel pattern and current profile fields have been inspected.

## Measurement

Review profile calls, website clicks, direction requests where applicable and genuine lead origin
quarterly. Changing a service area is not a promise of stronger Maps ranking: proximity, relevance
and prominence remain distinct from organic website visibility.
