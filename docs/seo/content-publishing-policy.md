# Content Publishing Policy

## Published

Requires a clear intent, unique useful copy, verified material facts, owner-compatible operations, metadata, canonical, one H1, internal links, appropriate schema, working CTA and successful automated/browser QA. Published pages may enter navigation and sitemap.

## Noindex

Used only when an existing URL must remain accessible or offers limited user utility but should not compete in search. It must emit `noindex, follow`, stay out of sitemap and primary navigation, and avoid unsupported claims.

## Draft

Drafts are registry data or documentation only. They are non-routable in production and absent from navigation, internal link hubs, schema and sitemap.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Owner-supplied current operational evidence.
2. Legal/company records and signed/approved documents.
3. Official government or manufacturer sources for external facts.
4. Genuine work records and consented media.
5. Existing website copy only as a lead for verification, never proof by itself.

## Automatic rejection

Reject copied/AI-scaled locality pages, swapped place names, unsupported brand pages, invented projects, fake reviews, synthetic project photography, stale price/premium claims, address misrepresentation, hidden keywords, generic “SEO text” and pages without a distinct user purpose.

## Change control

Every publication-state change must update the central registry, inventory, evidence register and changelog, then pass tests, lint, build, production crawl and representative browser checks.
